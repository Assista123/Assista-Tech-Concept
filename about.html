About us

Practical tools for real operational work.

Assista Tech Concept builds digital solutions for businesses. We focus on the everyday friction of managing records, workflows and customer interactions.

Based in Nigeria.

Our origin story

Assista Tech Concept started from a simple observation: much of the business software on offer is not built around how Nigerian businesses actually operate. Founded in February 2026 by Chiemerie Chinedu, the company set out to address this gap.

Too often, a business ends up changing its own way of working to fit a rigid system designed for a different market. We build tools that fit the work instead, tailored to the specific problem rather than adapted from a generic template.

We start with the problem, not the technology.

Many digital projects start with a preferred technology — a new framework, a trending tool, an app — and then look for a problem to apply it to. We take the opposite approach.

We look at how a business actually operates. Where do records get lost? What takes too much time? Where do customers drop off? Only after understanding the operational reality do we decide what to build.

Sometimes the answer is a conversational assistant. Sometimes it is a structured back-office platform. Sometimes it is just a better website. The tool should fit the workflow, not the other way around.

“If a system does not make the day-to-day work easier, it will not be used. We build for the people doing the work.” — Assista Tech Concept

How we work

1. Understand the friction

We look at how the business operates today, finding where time is lost or data is scattered.

2. Design the system

We map the minimum set of steps that removes the friction and fits the way the team already works.

3. Deliver and hand over

We build, test with real cases, and leave the team with something they can use immediately.

Let’s look at your workflow.

If you are managing records, coordinating a team, or handling customer enquiries, and it feels harder than it should be, let’s talk about it.
