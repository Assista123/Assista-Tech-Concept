Case study

Lead-qualification chatbot for a cable manufacturer

A website assistant that answers product and location questions first, then gently collects the details the sales team actually needs before handing the conversation over.

Client type
Electrical cable manufacturer
Channel
Website chatbot with WhatsApp hand-off
Focus
Product answers + lead qualification

The friction

The company received a steady mix of product questions, dealer location requests, and quote enquiries. Many of these arrived incomplete — missing quantity, location, or a clear sense of what the buyer actually needed.

Sales time was spent chasing basic details that could have been collected earlier. Serious buyers and casual browsers looked the same until someone followed up.

The approach

We built a scoped website chatbot that does three things in order:

  1. Answers product and dealer questions accurately from verified information.
  2. When buying intent is clear, gently collects need, quantity and location (and, optionally, a name).
  3. Only then offers a WhatsApp hand-off that already carries a clean summary for the sales team.

The WhatsApp button stays quiet until the lead is qualified. The bot does not invent specifications or push for a sale.

What changed

Enquiries that reach the sales team now arrive with the core details already filled in. The team starts with context instead of a blank conversation.

  • Product and dealer questions are handled on the website without immediate human involvement.
  • Quantity and location are collected before the hand-off.
  • The WhatsApp conversation opens with a clean summary, so follow-up is faster and more precise.

The same pattern works for other manufacturers that receive mixed product questions and commercial enquiries.
