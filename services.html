Services

Systems that sit inside real work, not on top of it.

We build three kinds of tools. Each one is designed to remove a specific kind of friction that slows teams down or leaves money and information on the table.

01

Conversational systems

Web and messaging interfaces that answer common questions, collect the right details, and hand a clean summary to the person who needs to act.

These are not open-ended chatbots. They are scoped to a clear job: qualify a buyer, log a farm activity, guide a routine process, or answer product and location questions accurately.

  • Lead qualification before the sales team is involved
  • Operational logging inside WhatsApp or Telegram
  • Guided flows that reduce incomplete enquiries
Example A cable manufacturer’s website chatbot that answers product questions, then collects need, quantity and location before offering a WhatsApp hand-off with the full summary already prepared.
02

Process & payment automation

Tools that remove repetitive checking and manual status updates from payments, contributions and routine records.

We connect the places information already arrives (bank alerts, email, forms) to the place the team needs to see it (a dashboard, a sheet, a status field).

  • Automatic matching of payments to members or invoices
  • Status updates driven by reference tags and email content
  • Clear records without daily manual reconciliation
Example An Ajo/Esusu contribution system where members generate a reference tag, pay into the group account, and the organiser’s bank alert is read automatically so the member’s dashboard flips from Pending to Paid.
03

Custom operational tools

Purpose-built software for specific workflows that do not fit off-the-shelf products well.

These are usually internal or semi-internal systems: inventory and flock tracking, cost and margin views, multi-role access for farm units or branches, or simple admin portals that keep records consistent.

  • Farm and inventory management
  • Role-based access for teams
  • Records that support daily decisions instead of living only in notebooks or chat
Example A multi-role poultry farm system covering flocks, feed, drugs, stock, sales and basic cost/margin views, used by both field staff and the person who needs the numbers.

Not sure which type fits?

Describe the friction you are dealing with. We will tell you whether a conversational system, automation, or a custom tool is the better starting point — or if none of them are needed yet.
