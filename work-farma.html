Case study

Farma Assista

A conversational farm-management assistant that helps poultry farmers record daily activities, monitor flock health and understand farm performance through WhatsApp and Telegram.

Client type
Poultry farming
Channel
WhatsApp and Telegram
Focus
Farm records, flock management and health checks

The operational friction

Poultry farming involves many small but important daily activities: recording feed consumption, tracking mortality, monitoring egg collection, administering medication and keeping financial records.

When these records are scattered across notebooks, memory and messaging conversations, it becomes harder to maintain a clear view of what is happening on the farm.

Farma Assista brings these activities into a conversational interface, allowing farmers to interact with their farm records without having to learn a separate, complicated system.

A familiar way to manage farm activities

Rather than asking farmers to navigate a complex dashboard, Farma Assista uses familiar messaging channels to guide them through common farm tasks.

After onboarding, farmers can access a menu of activities and record information through guided conversations.

  • Feed consumption and daily farm activity
  • Mortality and flock records
  • Egg collection and bird weight
  • Medication and litter checks
  • Sales, expenses and financial summaries
  • Stock, vaccination and flock health checks

Recording daily activity and mortality

Farmers can record routine activities and report flock losses through guided prompts. The system captures details such as the date, flock, number of birds lost and suspected cause.

The resulting record helps the farmer keep track of losses and the estimated financial impact, while confirming the updated flock count.

Vaccination reminders and flock management

Farma Assista also supports routine flock-management activities, including stock records, health checks and vaccination reminders.

Reminders help farmers keep important activities visible and maintain a record of their flock-management routine.

Symptom-based health checks

Farmers can describe visible symptoms and use the health-check flow to explore possible conditions associated with those signs.

The assistant presents possible matches as guidance for further investigation, not as a confirmed veterinary diagnosis.

Because different conditions can require different treatments, farmers are advised to consult a veterinarian before treating their birds. Severe cases warrant prompt veterinary attention.

Understanding farm performance

Farm records become more useful when farmers can review them together. Farma Assista brings revenue and expense information into a financial summary, helping farmers see how their farm operations are performing over a selected period.

The summary brings together sales revenue and recorded costs such as feed, medication and other expenses.

What Farma Assista brings together

The product connects everyday poultry-farming activities in one conversational experience.

Daily farm records

Feed, mortality, egg collection, weight, medication and litter checks.

Flock management

Flock records, stock information and vaccination reminders.

Health checks

Symptom-based guidance with clear recommendations to seek veterinary confirmation.

Sales and finance

Revenue, expenses and financial summaries for farm activities.
